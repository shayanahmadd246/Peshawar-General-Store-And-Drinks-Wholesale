The Problem

Traditional wholesale still runs on phone calls, WhatsApp threads, and handwritten lists. Shopkeepers struggle to confirm stock and MOQs; wholesalers lose track of approvals; delivery and invoices live in separate silos. Errors multiply when language and urgency collide.

Our Solution

One intelligent platform connects shopkeepers, wholesalers, admins, and delivery partners. Multilingual AI drafts carts from live inventory; humans still confirm checkout. Tenant isolation, dispatch, invoices, payments, and email alerts keep the full order lifecycle in sync.
